Cowboy Runner Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Cowboy Runner is a mobile game. This policy explains what data is used when you play and who it is shared with.

Data stored by the game itself

The game has no user accounts and does not send your games to any server of ours. Your high scores, coins and nuggets, in-game purchases and settings (volume, character and map) are stored only on your device. If you uninstall the game, they are deleted.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads from Google AdMob: an optional ad to keep playing after a crash and, from time to time, an ad between runs. To show ads and measure how they perform, Google may collect and use data from your device, such as the advertising ID, the IP address (used to estimate an approximate location), device and app information, and interactions with ads. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your permission before using this data for personalized ads, and you can change your choice from the music button (♪) → “Privacidad de los anuncios” (ad privacy).

More information: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. You can reset or turn off the advertising ID in your device settings.

Purchases (Google Play)

Real-money purchases (coins, nuggets, “No ads” and bundles) are made through Google Play. Google processes the payment; we never receive or store your card details. The game only receives the purchase confirmation so it can deliver what you bought.

Children

The game is intended for people aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

Security and retention

Communications with Google (ads and purchases) are encrypted. We do not keep personal data on servers of our own.

Your rights and contact

Since we do not keep your data on our own servers, you can delete everything by uninstalling the game. For any question about this policy, write to us at: cowboyrunner.soporte@protonmail.com.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will publish the new version on this same page with its date.
