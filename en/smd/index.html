MAPP Enterprise IA

A page that moves like the platform

The MAPP Enterprise IA page presents the AI-powered management platform with its own hand-made animations:

  • A 34-node neural network with pulses of light travelling between nodes.
  • A 3D “control tower” with oscillating bars and a curve that draws itself.
  • A radar sweeping the four regional offices: North, South, West and Centre.
  • A heartbeat line that pulses under “At SMD, technology never rests”.

Mobile App

An interactive demo of the app, inside the website

The Mobile App page doesn’t show a video of the app: it has a replica you can use. Visitors try the technicians’ app before installing it:

  • Switch between Agenda, Visits, Executions and Messages.
  • Filter the agenda by day, month or pending and move between dates.
  • Type in the chat with coordination and get a reply.
  • Play a voice note and see the photo evidence of a visit.

The project

The digital face of a maintenance company

Su Mano Derecha S.A.S. provides building and equipment maintenance and minor civil works for companies across Colombia, with over 20 years of experience and regional offices in Bogotá, Medellín, Cali and Barranquilla.

I designed and built its landing page: the company home, two product pages (MAPP Enterprise IA and the technicians’ Mobile App) and a forms portal for field staff. The goal: build trust and make requesting a quote one tap away.
