How it started

From a custom app to a product

Bóveda started as an app built for a collector with more than 3,000 banknotes: sync across three devices, photos in the cloud and AI that recognizes a banknote from a photo. With what I learned, I redesigned it from scratch so any collector can use it.

The custom app

  • Two-way sync between a tablet and two phones
  • FastAPI server with Postgres and photos on Cloudflare R2
  • OpenAI-powered AI that analyzes the photo and warns if the banknote is already there

Bóveda

  • New design: ink, gold, guilloche and frosted glass
  • No account or server, so it ships with no fixed costs
  • Google Drive backup and, later on, a paid version with its own cloud
