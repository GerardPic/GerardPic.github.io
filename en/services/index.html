04FAQ

Common questions

Do you work with clients abroad?

Yes. I work remotely, in English or Spanish, from Colombia (GMT−5, the same as New York in winter). Meetings are by video call.

How does payment work?

50% when you approve the proposal and 50% on delivery. Longer projects are split into phases. I accept bank transfers and international payment platforms.

Can I change the content later?

Yes. You get a way to edit text, images or map places without touching code, or I do it for you on the care plan.

I already have a Framer or Webflow site. Can you add to it?

Yes. The interactive map and other custom features can be built into your current site without rebuilding it.

What do I need to get started?

Your logo, an idea of what you want to say and, if you have them, copy and photos. If not, I'll help you define them.
