Horses and POWER UP

The horseshoe puts you on a horse that jumps higher and doubles your coins. Add the elixir for an unstoppable turbo gallop!

🤠

Cowboys and critters

Jesse Dust, Halcón Veloz, Gecko Kid, Sheriff Steel, Gus Pepita, Black Jack, Coyote Joe, Don Burro, Rocco Antifaz, Grizzly Bill and Toro Bravo.

🗺️

Four maps

The canyon at sunset, the mining camp at night, the snowy pass and the Mexican village fiesta, each with its own obstacles.

🎩

Dress your way

Hats, boots, suspenders, glasses, capes, guitars, spurs, trails and colorful horses for every character.

🎵

Original music

A song for every character and Wild West sound effects: gallops, crashes and neighs.

👆

Simple controls

Swipe sideways to switch lanes, up to jump and down to duck. Played with one finger.
