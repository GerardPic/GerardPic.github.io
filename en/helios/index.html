The Ember Palm

Block at the right moment and the enemy shot becomes your energy: defence, healing and ability charge at once.

🔥

Heat, not ammo

The Fénix-7 rifle overheats instead of running dry. Vent it in time… or use the heat to stop the machines.

🌈

Three frequencies

Ion, Fusion and Void. Each enemy gives way to one frequency, so you switch weapons with purpose.

🔆

Plasma giants

Ascua, Escoria and Corista: Burning Ones up to 3.5 metres tall, born from a wounded star. You read them by colour.

🪐

Zero gravity

Descend 88 m down the reactor shaft jumping between anchors, dodging spinning collector arms and plasma columns.

⚔️

Three sides

The Choir and the Halcyon corporation also fight each other. Provoke their clashes and take advantage.

Story

Year 2487. The stars are alive.

01

The Blackout

The Halcyon corporation tried to tame Helios, the star of the Tyr-Kael system. Helios answered: a plasma ejection wiped out the Nueva Aurora colony in eleven minutes.

02

The Choir

From the wounded corona came the Burning Ones, giants of living plasma. Halcyon blamed the Choir and erased every trace of its experiment.

03

Teo “Ash” Varga

He survived at nine, but a shard of living plasma lodged next to his heart: the Ember. Today he is the only soldier able to absorb the fire of a star.

At night, the Ember whispers to him in a language he doesn’t understand. He has never reported it.

How it’s made

Handmade, measured on the phone

Godot 4.7 with GDScript, Jolt physics, a modular level kit built in Blender, scanned materials per zone and dynamic resolution to keep 30 fps on a mid-range phone. Touch controls designed from day one.
