The brand

Colombian pajamas with soul

Margaritas is a Colombian pajama brand that brings together femininity, comfort and elegance. It was born to accompany those intimate moments when the noise fades and only what matters remains: your calm, your body, the way you feel.

It works with fabrics that caress, like premium satin and fresh cotton, in silhouettes designed for real bodies, from S to XXL. It is a local business: every piece is carefully sewn, stitch by stitch, ordered through WhatsApp and shipped all over Colombia.

Its styles

  • Samantha SatínSatin with a fluid drape and soft sheen
  • Samantha AlgodónFresh and soft for every day
  • GeraldineBlouse with coquette details
  • AccessoriesSatin scrunchies and bonnets

Editorial design

Collage with cursor parallax, a spinning badge, crossed ribbons that react to scrolling and a manifesto that inks in word by word.

🛍️

Full shop

Catalogue with filters, sorting, a quick view of each product, galleries per fabric design and FAQs.

💬

WhatsApp orders

A cart saved in the browser that builds the message with every item, size, quantity and total. No forms.

🌼

Brand identity

A twelve-petal daisy mark, a Playfair wordmark and logo versions for social media, labels and packaging.

✨

Animations

An opening curtain with a hand-drawn bow, a horizontally scrolling “How to order” section, 3D cards and a companion cursor.

⚡

Fast and accessible

Photos optimised as WebP in several sizes, and “reduce motion” respected across the whole site.
